ACCOUNT DELETION

How to delete your Fables account and data:

  • In-app (recommended): Open Fables → Settings → scroll to Danger Zone → tap Delete account. You will be asked to confirm your password. This immediately deletes your Firebase account and all locally stored data.
  • By email: If you cannot access the app, send a deletion request to d.iris.devstudio@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include the email address associated with your account. We will process your request within 7 days.

What gets deleted:

  • Your Firebase Authentication account (email, login credentials)
  • Your profile data (display name, profile photo)
  • Your world/catalog entries and watch history
  • Your activity feed posts
  • Your chat messages
  • All locally stored app data on your device

What may be retained: Aggregated, anonymized analytics data that cannot be linked back to you may be retained for service improvement purposes.
